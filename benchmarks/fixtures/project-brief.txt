ALPINE STUDIO — PROJECT BRIEF

Purpose
Create a small illustrated guide for visitors planning a weekend in the mountains. The guide should work on a phone and as a printed handout.

Delivery
The first draft is due on 14 September. The final guide is due on 21 September. Mira owns the illustrations; Leo owns the copy; Sam reviews the exported files.

Scope
Include an arrival checklist, one walking route, a weather note, and a packing list. The guide will contain six pages. The map is optional.

Review
Collect comments in one shared review document. Resolve factual corrections before polishing the illustrations. Keep the previous draft available for comparison.

Export
Deliver an editable source document and a PDF. Use the filename alpine-guide-final for the final export. Check that all pages open and that text remains readable at phone width.
